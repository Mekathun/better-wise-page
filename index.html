Better WiSE Pagebeta 0.1.1-beta1

An extension for the WiSE home page. It puts your next class and a to-do list made from course announcements at the top, opens the timetable where you are, and makes site search faster. This build is for people helping test it, so expect the odd rough edge.

Download

Download for Edge zip · 208 KB .crx instead

Take the zip. Browsers refuse a .crx that arrives from a download link, so the zip is the one that installs.

  1. Unzip it somewhere permanent. The folder you end up with must have manifest.json at the top.
  2. Open edge://extensions and switch on Developer mode in the left sidebar.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick that folder.
  4. Open wise.wesleycollege.net and refresh. The page rebuilds itself; the toolbar button opens the settings.

What it needs

Your own WiSE login in the same browser. The extension has no account and no server.

What it does with data

Course pages are read on your machine and kept in your browser profile. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on the AI features with your own API key.
