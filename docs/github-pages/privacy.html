Better WiSE Page

Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026 · Last updated 15 November 2026

Better WiSE Page changes how the Wesley College WiSE portal looks: one panel for today, a week timetable you can open, and a search box. It collects nothing about you. This page describes what the extension does with the pages it reads, and what it stores on your own machine.

Summary

  • The extension does not collect personal data.
  • There is no developer server, no account and no analytics.
  • Everything it reads stays in your browser tab and your own browser storage.

What it reads

It runs only on wise.wesleycollege.net. On that site it reads what is already on the page while you are signed in: class names, timetable periods, course announcements and their due dates. That text is used to draw the cards and the summary panel, and it stays inside the page.

It does not read your password, other tabs, browsing history, the clipboard or local files. It does not place tracking cookies. It is not injected into any other website.

What it stores

LocationYour own browser profile, through chrome.storage.local.
ContentsYour display settings: theme, animation speed, cards per row, background choices, and which cards you hid. Plus a local cache of pages you already opened, so the panel can draw before the site finishes loading.
Server storageNone. The developer has no backend and receives nothing.
RemovalClear the caches in the extension settings, or uninstall the extension. Either one deletes the stored data.

Network requests

The extension talks to one host: wise.wesleycollege.net, using the session you already have. It sends no data anywhere else. It includes no remote code; every script ships inside the package.

What it never does

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email address.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising.
  • No sale, rental or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No reading of pages outside the WiSE site.

Student data

The extension is used by students, and course content may concern minors. That content is displayed in your browser and nowhere else. The developer cannot see it.

Your control

Turn the panel off, clear the caches in settings, or uninstall the extension. Nothing persists after that.

Changes to this policy

If the extension's behaviour changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it and the release notes say what changed.
